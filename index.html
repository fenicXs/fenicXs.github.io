302 FPS FP16 vs 131 FPS FP32

VisDrone detection and DeepStream pipeline

Trained YOLOv8 on drone footage, exported it to ONNX and ran it in NVIDIA DeepStream. The FP16 TensorRT engine decodes 1080p H.264 and returns detections at 302 FPS on an RTX 5070 Laptop GPU. Moving the model input from 640 to 960 raised YOLOv8n mAP50 from 0.309 to 0.401. YOLOv8s at 960 reached 0.478 on validation and 0.389 on test-dev.

24 → 34 of 42 judged correct, dev set

Procurement Copilot

An agentic RAG system over federal acquisition documents. An LLM router chooses between Qdrant hybrid search with cross-encoder reranking, DuckDB SQL and a knowledge graph, and retries retrieval up to twice before it abstains. Generated SQL must pass a SELECT-only check and a table allowlist. After retrieval, SQL and graph fixes, an independent LLM judge scored 34 of 42 correct on the dev set, up from 24, and 12 of 15 on a held-out set, up from 8.

0.855 Dice SIIM-ACR pneumothorax, validation

Chest X-ray analysis with Swin Transformers

Segmentation, detection and classification models for chest radiographs. Test-time augmentation, checkpoint averaging and threshold tuning lifted pneumothorax Dice from 0.838 to 0.855, within about 1.5% of the first-place Kaggle solution. The classifier reached 0.829 mean AUC on the official ChestX-ray14 test split, and the detector reached 91.6% sensitivity at 1 false positive per image on TBX11K.
